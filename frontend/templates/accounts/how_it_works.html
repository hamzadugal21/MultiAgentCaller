How HireFlow AI works

A clearer hiring journey
for everyone.

HireFlow AI brings candidates, recruiters, and companies into one secure and structured recruitment workflow.

The journey

Five steps, one connected process.

From publishing an opportunity to reviewing interview outcomes, every stage stays clear and organized.

  1. 01

    Companies create roles

    Recruiters create and publish clear job opportunities.

  2. 02

    Candidates build profiles

    Candidates verify their account, upload a résumé, and add skills.

  3. 03

    Candidates apply

    Applications are managed securely in one place.

  4. 04

    Interviews are organized

    Recruiters send invitations and candidates confirm their interview schedule.

  5. 05

    Recruiters review outcomes

    Structured interview workflows support human recruiter review.
